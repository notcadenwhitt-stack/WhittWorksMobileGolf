Frequently asked questions

How much space do you need?

Our enclosure is roughly 15 ft deep, 14 ft wide, and 11 ft tall. We'll confirm your space works during booking — garages, ballrooms, gyms, parking lots, and backyards all do.

Do you need power?

One standard outlet within reach, or nothing at all — we can bring silent battery power for venues without it.

What if it rains?

The simulator moves indoors easily. If your venue can't host it inside, we'll work with you to reschedule.

Do guests need golf experience?

None. The simulator has games and modes for total beginners, kids, and serious players alike.

How long does setup take?

We arrive about an hour before start time and are fully packed out within an hour of finishing.

How far will you travel?

Anywhere in Alabama. Travel beyond our home area may carry a mileage fee — your quote will spell it out.
