Frequently asked questions

How much space do you need?

Our enclosure runs about 15 feet deep, 14 feet wide, and 11 feet tall. Garages, ballrooms, gyms, parking lots, and backyards all work well. We'll confirm your exact space during booking so there are no surprises on event day.

Do you need power on site?

One standard outlet within about 100 feet of the setup area covers it. We bring a heavy-duty extension cord and run the power ourselves, so all you do is point us to the plug. If your venue has no power at all, mention it when you request a quote and we'll sort out the arrangements well before your date.

What happens if it rains?

The simulator moves indoors in minutes. If your venue has a covered space, we relocate there and keep the party going. If it doesn't, we'll work with you on a reschedule rather than call it off.

Do guests need golf experience?

None. The simulator runs games and difficulty settings built for total beginners right alongside modes serious players respect. Kids, first-timers, and low-handicap regulars all end up on the same leaderboard.

How long does setup and teardown take?

We arrive about an hour before your start time and are fully packed out within an hour of your last swing. You won't see a cable during the party.

How far will you travel?

Anywhere in Alabama. Events near our home base carry no travel fee. Farther trips may add a mileage charge, and we'll spell that out clearly before you book.

How many guests can play per hour?

An attendant runs rotations the whole time, so most groups get everyone through several swings over the course of the event. Expecting a big crowd or a tight window? Tell us the guest count when you request a quote. Closest-to-the-pin and long-drive formats move a line much faster than full rounds, and booking a longer window gives everyone a turn without rushing.

How do deposits and booking work?

We hold your date with a signed agreement and a deposit, both handled during the quote process. The remaining balance is due closer to your event date, and everything is spelled out before you sign.

Still curious? Ask us anything in a quote request.
