Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Who we are

This site is operated by Southern Drive Golf Co, a mobile golf simulator rental business run by WhittWorks Studios LLC and serving events across Alabama. This policy explains what information we collect through the site, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information you provide

When you submit a quote request, we collect your name, email address, event date and type, event city, guest count, and any notes you add. Phone number is optional. We only ask for what we need to price your event and get back to you.

Information collected automatically

We use PostHog (US Cloud), a product analytics tool, to understand how visitors use this site: which pages get viewed, how people navigate, and whether the quote form gets used. PostHog loads only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, or never respond to the banner, PostHog never loads and nothing is set. You can see exactly what PostHog stores, and for how long, on our Cookie Policy page.

We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent visitor data to anyone.

How we use it

Quote request details are used to prepare your event pricing, answer your questions, and coordinate the booking if you move forward. If you gave a phone number, we may call or text you about that specific booking; the consent language next to the phone field on the quote form covers this. Analytics data, collected only with consent, helps us see which pages are useful and where visitors get stuck, so we can improve the site.

How we share it

Quote form submissions are delivered to our inbox by Formspree, our third-party form processor. If the form is ever temporarily disabled, it sends nothing and says so on the page. We also work with a web hosting provider that serves these pages and keeps standard server logs. Neither Formspree nor our hosting provider is authorized to use your information for their own marketing. We do not share your information with any other business, and we do not sell it.

Security

Quote form submissions travel over an encrypted connection. We limit access to submitted information to the people who need it to plan your event. No online system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, standard precautions to protect what you send us.

Retention

We keep quote request details for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and, if you book with us, to deliver the event and handle any follow-up. After that, we remove information we no longer have a reason to hold. Analytics data collected with consent follows PostHog's standard retention period on its US Cloud service.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make any of these requests, use the quote form and note in the message field that it's a privacy request rather than an event inquiry, and we'll handle it directly rather than treat it as a booking. You can also withdraw analytics consent at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in the site footer.

Children under 13

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us through the quote form and we'll remove it.

External links

This site may link to third-party pages, such as social media profiles. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of sites we don't operate, so review their policies separately.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site or our practices change. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
