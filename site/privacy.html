Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

Last updated: 13 August 2026

1. Scope and Application

Southern Drive Golf Co ("we", "us", "our") is a trade name of WhittWorks Studios LLC. We rent a mobile golf simulator for events throughout the State of Alabama, including corporate functions, private parties, fundraisers, weddings, festivals, and school events. We transport the equipment to the venue you choose, set it up, staff it with an attendant, and remove it after the event. We do not operate a fixed venue.

This Privacy Policy governs this website (the "Site") and the information you ("you", "your") submit through it. It does not govern any website we do not operate, and it does not govern information you give us in person, by mail, or through a signed event agreement, except to the extent that information originated in a quote request submitted through the Site.

We operate in the United States and serve events in Alabama. We do not direct the Site to visitors outside the United States. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Site or submit a quote request.

2. Information You Provide to Us

The Site has one form: the quote request form. It is the only place on the Site where you can send us information. The Site has no user accounts, no logins, no passwords, and no payment processing, so we neither request nor receive credentials or card details through the Site.

The quote request form collects the following:

Field Required Why we ask
Name Required To address our reply and identify the request
Email address Required To send availability, pricing, and follow-up questions
Telephone number Optional To call or text you about the booking, subject to section 5 below
Event date Required To check availability for that date
Event type Required To match the setup and staffing to the occasion
Event city or town Required To determine travel and service coverage within Alabama
Standard power outlet within 100 feet Optional To plan power for the simulator at the venue
Approximate guest count Optional To estimate run time, staffing, and pricing
Notes Optional Free text for anything else you want us to know

The form also contains one hidden anti-spam field, commonly called a honeypot. Visitors do not see it and are not asked to complete it. It exists to identify automated submissions, and it collects no information about you.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the notes field. We do not need it, and we do not ask for it.

3. Information Collected Automatically

3.1 Analytics, and only with your consent

We use PostHog (US Cloud) for product analytics. PostHog is strictly consent-gated on this Site. The PostHog script is not requested, not loaded, and not executed until you click "Accept analytics" on the cookie banner. Until you do, the Site makes no contact with PostHog, sets no analytics cookie, and writes no analytics storage entry. If you decline the banner, or ignore it entirely, PostHog never loads and stores nothing. If you accept and later decline, PostHog stops capturing and the Site deletes PostHog's stored entries from your browser.

When you have accepted, PostHog records how visitors use the Site: pages viewed, navigation between pages, and interactions such as submitting the quote request form. It stores an identifier that distinguishes one browser from another. We do not run session recording, we do not run advertising or retargeting trackers, and we permit no third-party marketing pixels on the Site.

3.2 Storage set in your browser

The Site itself writes two localStorage entries, and only after you answer the cookie banner. If you never answer it, the Site stores nothing. The table below lists everything the Site and PostHog store. Our Cookie Policy covers the same ground in more detail.

Name Type Set by Purpose Duration

sdg-consent-at localStorage This Site, after you choose Records the date and time of that choice, in ISO format Until you clear it or change your choice
ph_<project_api_key>_posthog Cookie PostHog, only after you accept Holds a distinct ID and a session ID so repeat visits can be recognized 365 days
ph_<project_api_key>_posthog localStorage PostHog, only after you accept Holds feature flag state and analytics configuration Until you clear it or decline analytics, which deletes these entries

3.3 Hosting logs

Netlify, our hosting provider, serves these pages and may keep standard server logs as part of delivering and securing the Site. Such logs typically record information including without limitation the requesting IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, and the browser user agent. These logs are a function of web hosting rather than of anything we have configured for marketing, and we do not use them to build profiles of visitors.

4. Purposes of Processing

We process the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your quote request and answer questions about it.
  • To check availability for your date and prepare pricing for your event.
  • To coordinate and deliver the event if you proceed, including scheduling, travel, load-in, power and space planning, and on-site staffing.
  • To keep a record of the booking and handle any follow-up related to it.
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it, using analytics collected only with your consent.
  • To detect and reduce automated spam submissions.

A submitted quote request is a request, not a confirmed booking. We hold a date by signed agreement and deposit, both handled during the quote process, with the balance due closer to the event date. Information you provide in a quote request supports that process to the extent that you choose to continue with it.

5. Telephone Contact and SMS

The telephone number field is optional. Consent to be called or texted is not required to request a quote, and leaving the field blank has no effect on whether we respond or on the price we quote. If you do not provide a number, we reply by email.

By providing your telephone number, you agree that Southern Drive Golf Co may call or text you about your event booking and coordination. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages.

Contact by telephone or text relates to that specific booking only. We do not send marketing text messages, and we do not add your number to a promotional list.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We disclose it only as described in this section.

6.1 Service providers

  • Formspree, our form processor. The form transmits quote request submissions to Formspree, which delivers them to our email inbox. The form endpoint is not yet configured. While it remains unconfigured, the form displays a notice and transmits nothing.
  • Netlify, our hosting provider. Netlify serves the pages of the Site and may keep the server logs described in section 3.3.
  • PostHog, our analytics processor. PostHog receives usage data only after you accept analytics on the cookie banner, and receives nothing before that point.

These providers process information to supply their service to us. They are not authorized to use your information for their own marketing.

6.2 Legal compulsion and protection of rights

We may disclose information where the law requires it, including in response to a subpoena, court order, or other lawful request by a government authority, or where disclosure is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim, to enforce an agreement, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of any person.

7. Data Security

The Site transmits form submissions over an encrypted connection. We limit access to submitted information to the people who need it to quote and deliver your event. No method of transmission or storage on the internet is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you send information to us at your own discretion.

8. Data Retention

We keep quote request information for as long as necessary for the purposes described in section 4, which includes responding to your inquiry, delivering the event if you book with us, and handling follow-up related to that booking. When information is no longer necessary for those purposes, we remove it.

The two consent entries described in section 3.2 remain in your browser until you clear them or change your choice. PostHog storage follows the durations shown in that same table.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Analytics consent. You may withdraw or grant analytics consent at any time using the "Cookie preferences" control in the footer of every page. Declining stops PostHog from loading on future visits and deletes the entries it stored earlier.
  • Access and deletion. You may ask what quote request information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Submit the request through the quote request form and state in the notes field that it is a privacy request rather than an event inquiry, so that we handle it as such.
  • Telephone. You may leave the telephone field blank, and you may reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages.
  • Browser controls. Your browser settings let you block or clear cookies and localStorage. Blocking storage entirely may prevent the Site from remembering your consent choice, since that record is itself a localStorage entry.

9.1 California notice

We post this policy in accordance with the California Online Privacy Protection Act, which requires an operator of a commercial website to post a policy identifying the categories of information collected and the categories of third parties with whom it is shared. Sections 2, 3, and 6 provide those disclosures.

9.2 Browser privacy signals

Some browsers and extensions send a universal opt-out signal on your behalf. The Site honours two of them: Global Privacy Control, transmitted as the Sec-GPC header and the navigator.globalPrivacyControl property, and the older Do Not Track signal.

Where either signal is present, the Site treats it as a decision already made. Analytics is not loaded, no analytics cookie or storage entry is created, and the consent banner does not appear, because there is nothing further to ask. This behaviour applies whether or not you have previously accepted analytics, and it cannot be overridden from within the Site.

10. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted information to us, submit a request through the quote request form and we will delete it.

11. Third-Party Links

The Site may link to pages we do not operate, such as social media profiles. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of those sites. Review their policies separately before providing information to them.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy as the Site or our practices change. We post any revision on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Site after a revision is posted indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

13. How to Contact Us

Send privacy questions and requests through the quote request form on this Site, selecting "Not an event: a privacy or accessibility request" as the event type. Choosing that option removes the event-specific requirements from the form. We do not publish an email address or telephone number on the Site, which keeps both out of reach of automated harvesting. The form endpoint is not yet configured. While it remains unconfigured, the form displays a notice and transmits nothing.
