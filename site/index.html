How it works

1. Pick your date

Tell us when, where, and roughly how many guests. We travel to every corner of Alabama, indoors or out.

2. We build the course

Enclosure, screen, launch monitor, projector: we handle setup and breakdown, usually in under an hour each way.

3. Your guests play

A trained attendant runs the simulator from first swing to last. Real courses and games for every skill level, we host, you enjoy.

Why Alabama hosts choose us

Every booking comes with a trained attendant who runs the simulator from setup to the last swing. Live ball flight and real course data play out on a big screen inside an enclosure built for a full night of play. Guests show up as they are and start swinging within minutes, first-timers and scratch golfers alike. We're a small Alabama operation: the same person who answers your call runs your event.
