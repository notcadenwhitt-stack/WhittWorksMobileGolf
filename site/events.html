The events we book most

Corporate & team building

Product launches, client days, holiday parties, and trade show booths. Branded leaderboards give visitors a reason to stop and stay.

We arrive about an hour before your start time and set up out of the way, then an attendant runs every swing so nobody on your team has to staff it. Ballrooms, warehouse bays, and parking lots all work.

Popular for: Client entertainment, holiday parties, sales kickoffs, trade show booths

Fundraisers & galas

Closest-to-the-pin and long-drive contests raise real dollars for your cause, with an attendant handling every swing.

Contest formats move a line far faster than full rounds, so a crowd cycles through in the window you actually have. Set your own entry price and run as many rounds as the night allows.

Popular for: Charity galas, booster drives, church and civic fundraisers

Private parties

Birthdays, graduations, bachelor and bachelorette parties, and family reunions. Whatever the occasion, we bring the entertainment to it.

Guests walk up and start swinging within minutes, no lesson required. The simulator runs settings built for total beginners right next to modes serious players respect, so a first-timer and a scratch golfer end up on the same leaderboard.

Popular for: Birthdays, graduations, reunions, backyard parties
