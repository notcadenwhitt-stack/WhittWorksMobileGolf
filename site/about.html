About us

We started with a simple idea: everybody wants to swing a golf club, and almost nobody gets the chance at a party. So we built a golf course that travels, complete with the person who runs it.

We stay hands-on through the whole thing. We build the enclosure, run the launch monitor, and stick around for the entire event, so your guests get a host who runs the show from first swing to last.

The equipment matches that promise: a tour-grade launch monitor, real course simulation, and an enclosure built to handle a full night of swings without a wrinkle. We price it fair for what it is, a premium experience without a premium markup.

We're Alabama-based and Alabama-run, and we travel to every corner of the state, from Huntsville to Mobile.
