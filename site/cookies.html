Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies and localStorage are small pieces of data a website stores in your browser. Cookies get sent back to the server with each request; localStorage stays in the browser and is read only by scripts on the site that set it. Both let a site remember something between visits, like a choice you made or an anonymous ID.

What this site stores

Once you make a choice on the consent banner, this site stores two localStorage entries so it can remember that decision. If you never respond to the banner, nothing is stored at all. It sets PostHog's analytics cookie and localStorage entries only if you click Accept on the consent banner. If you decline, or never respond, PostHog never loads and none of its entries get created.

We don't use advertising or retargeting cookies, and we don't allow other companies to set cookies on this site for their own purposes.

How to change your choice

You can accept or decline analytics at any time using the "Cookie preferences" button in the footer of every page. Declining stops PostHog from loading on future visits and deletes any entries it stored earlier.

You can also control cookies and localStorage directly through your browser settings, which let you block, clear, or get notified about site storage. Blocking storage entirely may affect how the consent banner remembers your choice, since that itself relies on localStorage.
