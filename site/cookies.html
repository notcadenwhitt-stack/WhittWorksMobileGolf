1. Purpose of This Policy

Southern Drive Golf Co ("we", "us", "our"), a trade name of WhittWorks Studios LLC, operates this website (the "Site") and rents a mobile golf simulator for events throughout the State of Alabama. This policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage technologies the Site uses, who sets them, what they do, and how you ("you", "your") control them.

This policy addresses storage technologies only. Our Privacy Policy governs the information you submit through the quote request form and how we handle it. Read the two documents together. Where this policy describes a storage technology in detail, that description controls over any shorter summary of the same technology elsewhere on the Site.

2. What Cookies and Similar Technologies Are

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. Your browser returns that file to the server it came from with each subsequent request to the same domain, which allows the server to recognize a returning browser.

Browser local storage (technically the Web Storage API, and referred to below as "local storage") holds named values in your browser. Unlike a cookie, local storage is not attached to network requests. Only scripts served from the same website can read it, and it persists until a script or you remove it.

Both technologies store data on your device rather than on our servers, and neither grants access to files, contacts, or hardware on your device. This policy refers to them together as "storage technologies".

3. Our Approach to Consent

No analytics technology loads on the Site before you accept it. The PostHog analytics script is not requested, downloaded, or executed until you click "Accept analytics" on the consent banner. Until that moment, your browser makes no connection to PostHog, and no analytics cookie or local storage entry exists.

If you decline, or if you close or ignore the banner without answering, the result is the same: the script is never requested and PostHog sets nothing.

This approach is stricter than the common alternative, in which a site loads an analytics library on every page and then instructs it not to collect data. Under that arrangement the third-party script still runs in your browser and still contacts the vendor's servers. On this Site the script is never fetched at all unless you accept.

No general United States law requires cookie consent for a business of this type, and the Site serves visitors in the United States. We operate the banner as a voluntary practice.

5. Technologies We Do Not Use

The Site does not use, and does not permit any third party to place:

  • Advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • Third-party marketing or conversion pixels.
  • Cross-site or cross-device tracking of any kind.
  • Session recording, session replay, or capture of your keystrokes or screen.
  • Authentication or payment cookies, because the Site has no accounts, no logins, no passwords, and no payment processing.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or analytics data, and we do not permit other companies to set storage technologies on the Site for their own purposes.

6. Managing Your Choices

6.1 The cookie preferences control

The "Cookie preferences" control in the footer of every page reopens the consent banner, so you may change your answer at any time and as often as you wish.

If you withdraw consent, PostHog does not load on that page or on later visits, and the Site deletes the PostHog cookie and local storage entries it previously set.

6.2 Your browser controls

Your browser can block, inspect, and clear cookies and local storage for any website, including this one. The settings sit under privacy or site data in most browsers, and each browser's help documentation explains the exact steps.

6.3 Effect of clearing storage

Clearing site data removes the sdg-consent and sdg-consent-at records along with everything else, so the Site no longer knows what you chose and the banner appears again on your next visit. Blocking storage entirely produces the same result on every visit, since the record of your choice cannot persist.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy as the Site or the technologies it uses change. The effective date and the last updated date at the top of this page identify the current version, which supersedes any earlier version. We will post a material change here before it takes effect.

8. Contact

Direct any question about this policy through the quote request form on the Site. We do not publish an email address or telephone number, so the form is the route to us. Select "Not an event: a privacy or accessibility request" as the event type, and we will handle it accordingly.

The form transmits through Formspree, a third-party form processor, which delivers messages to our inbox. If the form is not configured to transmit, it displays a notice on the page and sends nothing.
