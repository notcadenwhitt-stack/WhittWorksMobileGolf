# Equipment Build-Outs — Three Tiers

Internal purchasing document, revised 2026-08-13 after an independent line-by-line review. Prices were verified on 2026-08-13 either by direct page fetch or by corroborating search results (each line links its actual source; items that resisted live verification are listed at the bottom). Sale prices move; re-check any line the day you order. All three tiers include the PopCaddie enclosure and quiet power. Transport (trailer or hauling) is deliberately excluded per plan.

**Common to all tiers, ordering notes:**
- Enclosure ships in 28 to 30 days via FedEx Air; customs averages ~$130 collected separately on delivery. Order it first. (Full enclosure facts: docs/research/verified-addenda.json.)
- The enclosure includes: electric pump, double-layer impact screen, ceiling projector bracket, custom logo printing on 3 sides, removable side panels, repair kit. Interior: 14.99 ft deep, 13.77 ft wide, 10.82 ft tall.
- Battery power is indoor-safe and near-silent. Gas generators are outdoor-only (carbon monoxide). The enclosure blower is the dominant continuous draw; runtime math assumes 500 to 800W total.
- **Laptop RAM rule: GSPro requires 16GB. Some retail RTX 4060 SKUs ship 8GB (the Best Buy Cyborg 15 SKU 6532164 slug says 8GB). Only buy a 16GB configuration; both tiers below budget for it.**
- **Daytime outdoor events change the projector choice.** Every tier here is bright enough indoors or after sunset. In daylight the enclosure's light blocking matters far more than lumens, and 4,000 is the sensible floor if daytime outdoor work is a real part of the business. Full math and upgrade options: `docs/PROJECTOR-BRIGHTNESS.md`.

## Value Build — target under $8,000

The honest budget reality: every launch monitor under $700 is Doppler radar, which sits behind the ball. The SC4 Pro fits the enclosure at exactly its spec (5 ft behind ball + vendor-recommended 10 ft ball-to-screen = 15 ft, the enclosure's depth) with the unit against the rear wall. It works, with zero slack. If you want breathing room, the camera-based swap below trades subscription cost for placement freedom.

Notes and swaps:
- Layout at spec: screen at the front wall, ball 10 ft back, SC4 Pro 5 ft behind the ball against the rear wall (docs/research/verified-addenda.json).

- The sub-$700 short-throw projector class no longer exists new; the TH671ST at $949.99 is today's cheapest true short-throw sim projector. The $599.99 standard-throw Optoma HD146X cannot fill the screen inside the enclosure's depth.
